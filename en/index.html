About

Look,
then wait for the light.

I'm Nicolas Cartalade, a photographer from Clermont-Ferrand, in the heart of Auvergne. Among age-old mountains, colourful skies and volcanic stones, I enjoy observing the world and capturing moments that touch my sensibility and move me.

I shoot with a Canon EOS R8, looking for sober images where piercing light and the enveloping atmosphere of the moment portray reality, freezing it on a canvas.
